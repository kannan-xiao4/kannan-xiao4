# Link icons

The original assets are stored locally and shown with their original aspect ratios and colors. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- GitHub: `github.svg`, from `GitHub Logos/SVG/GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg` in https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip . Official source and usage: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo
- X: `x.png`, official black mark at https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/logo-black.png.twimg.1920.png . Official source: https://about.x.com/es/who-we-are/brand-toolkit . Guide: https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-brand-guidelines.pdf
- LinkedIn: `linkedin.png`, from `in-logo/LI-In-Bug.png` in https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/xbu/linkedin-revised-brand-guidelines/logos/in-logo.zip . Official source and usage: https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads
- RSS: `rss.svg`, Google Material Symbols Outlined `rss_feed_24px.svg`, unchanged. Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/symbols/web/rss_feed/materialsymbolsoutlined/rss_feed_24px.svg . Apache License 2.0: see LICENSE.txt.
